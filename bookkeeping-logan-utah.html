Scope

Cleanup vs catch-up vs monthly

  • Catch-up — Missing months of work in the file.
  • Cleanup — Data present but wrong, duplicated, or unreconciled.
  • Monthly — Steady close once the file is reliable.

Many Cache Valley businesses need a cleanup/catch-up first — especially after a long DIY stretch — then monthly.
