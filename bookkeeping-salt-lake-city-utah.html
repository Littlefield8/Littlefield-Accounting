Scope

Cleanup vs catch-up vs monthly

  • Catch-up — Missing time: transactions not entered or not categorized for months.
  • Cleanup — Wrong or unreliable data: bad categories, duplicates, accounts that won't reconcile, a chart of accounts that doesn't match the business.
  • Monthly — Ongoing close once the file is trustworthy.

SLC businesses often need both catch-up and cleanup — especially when ecommerce or multiple bank feeds grew faster than the books. Assessment sets the scope.
