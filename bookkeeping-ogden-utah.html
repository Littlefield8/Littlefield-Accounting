Scope

Cleanup vs catch-up vs monthly

  • Catch-up — You're behind: months of transactions aren't entered or categorized. The work is filling the gap so the file is current.
  • Cleanup — Data is there but wrong or unreliable: bad categories, duplicates, accounts that won't reconcile, a chart of accounts that doesn't match how you operate.
  • Monthly — Ongoing close after the file is trustworthy: each month stays reconciled and report-ready.

Many Ogden businesses need both catch-up and cleanup before monthly makes sense. The assessment decides the mix.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to meet in person if I'm in Ogden?

No. Littlefield is based in Ogden but delivers bookkeeping through QuickBooks Online, phone, and email/messaging. Being down the street doesn't change the workflow — and you don't need an office visit for the same specialist close as clients elsewhere on the Wasatch Front.

How does remote bookkeeping actually work?

You keep using QuickBooks Online as you do now. Littlefield is invited as an accountant user; Kade and his team handle categorization, reconciliation, and reporting in your file. Calls and messages cover anything that needs a conversation.

What if I'm on QuickBooks Desktop, not Online?

Littlefield works in QuickBooks Online only. If you're on Desktop and want to work together, you'd need to migrate to Online first (or plan that migration). Happy to talk through whether migration makes sense for your situation.

Do I need cleanup before monthly bookkeeping?

Usually yes if you're more than about one to two months behind, or if reconciliations and reports aren't trustworthy. Starting monthly on a broken file just documents the mess faster.

Will you work with my CPA or tax preparer?

Yes. The goal is tax-ready P&L and Balance Sheet (and a file they can navigate). Littlefield does the books; your preparer files the return.

How is pricing determined?

Soft pricing: monthly fees reflect transaction volume and account count; cleanup/catch-up is flat-scoped after a QBO file review. No published dollar amounts here — quote after looking at your file.

How long does a typical cleanup take?

Light catch-ups are often weeks; deeper multi-year or heavily broken files take longer and may be phased. Owner response time matters.

Who actually does the work?

Kade and his team. Kade Littlefield, a QuickBooks Online ProAdvisor and owner, leads the work on your file.
