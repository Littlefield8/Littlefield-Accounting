Scope

Cleanup vs catch-up vs monthly

  • Catch-up — You're behind on entering or categorizing activity.
  • Cleanup — What's in the file is wrong or won't reconcile.
  • Monthly — Ongoing close after the file is trustworthy.

Utah County professional-services and small service firms often need a short cleanup before monthly feels useful — especially after a DIY stretch or a bookkeeper change.
