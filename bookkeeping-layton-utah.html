Scope

Cleanup vs catch-up vs monthly

  • Catch-up — Months of activity never entered or never categorized.
  • Cleanup — Activity is in QBO but wrong, duplicated, or unreconciled.
  • Monthly — Ongoing close once the file is trustworthy.

Contractors and service businesses in Layton often need both — catch-up for the busy months that got skipped, cleanup for categories and reconciliations that drifted.

No. Even though Ogden is close, bookkeeping runs through QuickBooks Online, phone, and email/messaging. No office visit required.

Littlefield is Online-only. You'd need to migrate to QuickBooks Online to work together.

Often yes — especially after a busy season when categorization and card reconciliations slipped. Assessment confirms whether catch-up, cleanup, or both come first.

Yes. Tax-ready books are the handoff; Littlefield doesn't file your return.
