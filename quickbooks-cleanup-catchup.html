Common triggers

Reasons clients usually reach out for this.

Behind on reconciliation

DIY bookkeeping got away

QuickBooks Online was set up with good intentions, but categorization and upkeep fell behind over time.

Switched bookkeepers

Frequently Asked Questions

How far behind can my books be before cleanup?

There's no cutoff — cleanup engagements have covered anywhere from a few disorganized months to multiple years of unreconciled QuickBooks Online data.

How long does a cleanup take?

It depends on how far behind the books are and how many accounts and transactions are involved — this gets scoped on the free consultation call before any work begins.

What happens after cleanup is finished?

Most clients move directly into monthly bookkeeping so the books stay accurate going forward instead of falling behind again.
